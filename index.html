Hardware Troubleshooting

High-End Gaming PC Restart Issue (Intel Core i9-14900KF)

Received a high-end gaming PC with constant random restarts. The previous repair shop initially identified the issue as a faulty motherboard.

  • Observed consistent random restarts during normal usage and system idle.
  • Verified motherboard functionality by testing with a known working 14th gen CPU.
  • Checked RAM, SSD, and PSU, and performed a CMOS reset to eliminate configuration issues.
  • Tested with an alternative power supply to rule out power-related problems.
  • Updated BIOS and monitored system behavior for stability improvements.
  • Adjusted CPU voltage settings (undervolting) to test for power or thermal instability.
  • Performed a clean OS reinstallation to eliminate possible software or driver-related causes.
  • Performed CPU cross-testing using another 14th gen processor, which stabilized the system.
  • Identified the processor as the root cause after systematic elimination of all other components.
  • Verified with Intel support that the unit was part of a known faulty batch and eligible for warranty replacement.
Result: Confirmed a faulty i9-14900KF processor, verified known batch issues with Intel, and resolved the problem through warranty replacement.
